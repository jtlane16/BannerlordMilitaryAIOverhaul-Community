# Autonomous enemy-siege acceptance

Load the same disposable wartime save in the armed build and let world-map time run. No commands or target selection are needed. Return the single **MAIO campaign acceptance** popup with its run ID. The observer has a limit of 360 campaign hours or 15 real minutes.

**Pass:** after arming, a production army selects a legal enemy fortification, receives native `BesiegeSettlement` with no target camp at departure, reaches a native camp at that fortification, and the same army starts a native assault. An issued order, a pre-existing or fixture camp, or native assault selection before the battle starts is not a pass. No capture, defense diversion, or repulse is inferred from an assault. If they happen, production logs them separately. If the campaign cannot exercise the whole chain, the popup reports `INCONCLUSIVE` and the furthest observed step. Neither completion nor timeout changes any production orders.

## What the headless suite proves

- **Selection and native order:** existing viable armies before fresh purchases; nearer viable castle over a higher-value town; detour over straight-line distance and native value on a route tie; unreachable and opted-out forces rejected. New siege orders require a current kingdom foothold within two land fortification links. `Siege selected` records `frontierAnchor` and `frontierLinks`; `outside-two-fief-frontier` identifies rejected targets or closed pre-camp assignments. A third-link target nearer the muster than any legal target remains ineligible until a new foothold puts it in range. The installed `GetBesiegeBehavior` follows the issued order to the gate, holds at an unprepared camp, and selects assault given preparation and a stubbed favorable readiness decision.
- **Muster and authority:** 51 influence at a 250 balance is too expensive, 50 is legal; summons are not present strength. Reload keeps the original formation, paid summons, and reserve. Existing home and allied suites retain the player and native battle checks. One or two sieging armies divert when needed, a paid muster can divert intact, and an otherwise unroutable defense can release the necessary formations and gather a viable replacement from their real positions.
- **Sustainable camp and withdrawal:** a camp stays after losses below the new-siege threshold and discovery of a closer fief. Only hostile field troops actually local, visible from camp, and able to reach the land fight count. The smoke tests distinguish camp radius from gate radius and sight range; count attached and independent forces once; exclude interior and unrelated battle-held forces; and verify that 600 against 500 does not lift while 601 can, but never during a battle. An independent remaining besieger keeps the native camp. Starvation, peace, and ownership change are not repulses.
- **Repulse and reuse:** successful local-threat withdrawal and a native outside-battle defeat followed by a real camp-end callback establish separate cooldowns. A different army cannot retry on day six or after loading a pre-expiry save. Day seven still requires normal strength, a route and no currently observed overwhelming contact. The saved former campsite is a search location, not a saved enemy sighting; the review uses the new leader's actual spotting range. Capture and response-led relief may reuse the same formation only for a viable native one-ring objective; the same retry gate also applies to the fixed named follow-on.
- **One-shot observer:** exercises atomic claim, no pass for a camp or an order alone, matching assault evidence, timeout, and exactly one popup across a stale observer reload. Headless map positions and encounter states are fixtures. They are not live campaign evidence.

## Coordinator

Use the acceptance build and mark it with `acceptance-siege-v1.armed` containing `siege-v1 <fresh lowercase 32-character GUID>`. Install via `tests/InstallAcceptance.ps1 -Scenario siege-v1 -Package <directory> -ExpectedHash <sha256>` with the game and launcher closed. Keep the previous receipts. The ordinary DLL runs the same production planner without the observer or controlled attack fixture.

Production is land-routed; it does not plan naval sieges. The commander uses Bannerlord's spotting range for the actual camp observer and the native nearby-defense radius. It does not model a separate scouting network, create wars, start camps remotely, or issue assault orders. Save compatibility checks read the native behavior's saved references and scalar data; only the armed campaign can confirm a live assault on the disposal save.
