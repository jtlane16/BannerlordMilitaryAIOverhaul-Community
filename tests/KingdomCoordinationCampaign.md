# Kingdom-wide coordination acceptance

Load the same disposable wartime save with `coordination-v1` armed and let the world map run. No commands, target selection, or follow-up setup are required. Return the single **MAIO campaign acceptance** popup and its run ID. The observer ends within 360 campaign hours or 15 real minutes.

## What counts in campaign

A pass needs one responding kingdom, a real production decision about competing objectives, and at least two distinct commanded armies executing the decision **at the same time** under matching native orders. The observer reads the decision's actual route candidates and separately checks the current relief and siege registries, native orders, and party rosters. A qualifying decision can be:

- **Split defenses:** to serve both still-eligible objectives, a second army has a real route to the lower defense and is executing it while the selected army defends the higher objective.
- **Joint defense:** neither selected force could provide adequate committed coverage alone, both are executing the higher defense together, and a routable lower defense is still eligible but unassigned by that kingdom.
- **Joint understrength defense transfer:** the whole selected group (at least two armies) had native orders for the same still-eligible lower defense and still had a route to it when the decision was made. All are now executing native orders for the higher defense in the same snapshot, and none is assigned to the lower one. This proves a real tradeoff even if their combined force is understrength. A fresh understrength group or an abandoned defense with no remaining route is not enough.
- **Defense diversion:** a new allied defense takes one army out of a pair of previously issued siege orders and the other siege remains in production under its original order; or both sieges yield because the defense needs both. A home siege also recalls surplus offense; recall by itself isn't evidence of a joint response.

Different kingdoms, a proposal, a muster without a departure, a single response, two unrelated offensives, and orders observed only in separate snapshots do not pass. Duplicate party claims in the contemporaneous production registries fail the audit. A valid response may still be understrength or fail to save a settlement. The run reports **INCONCLUSIVE** if the live world does not exercise a qualifying case. The summary includes the competing choices watched, kingdoms with multiple formations, and how far the best observed case got. The observer never creates an objective or formation, adjusts diplomacy, moves a party, or cancels any production order. The exact deterministic east/west geometry and individual withdrawal cases are separate headless evidence, not implied by the popup.

## What the headless suite proves

- **Lageta/Amitatys saved-order regression:** modeled after `d29a43ad`. Begin with Hecaron covering Lageta and Saratis assigned to Amitatys. Reload with Saratis next to Lageta and Hecaron's order cleared in an unrelated field battle. The first planning pass sends Saratis back without paying; a river detour reverses the decision. After the battle Hecaron's defend order is restored, both stay while needed, and once Hecaron suffices only Saratis goes farther. On a real replay compare `Relief route match` (proposal and route ETAs), `Relief departure` (actual order), `Relief order state` (assignment, native order/contact and lock), and `Relief execution`/`Relief review` (physical field strength). A proposal or nearby banner does not establish relief success.

- **Interrupted relief roster (`5d4a6825`):** reload with Hecaron fleeing and his party order cleared. Saratis returns to Lageta. Hecaron engages the besieger; Saratis pursues an unrelated party. Each army has an attached lord who could lead an Amitatys muster via an open fort. While neither army provides ready coverage, both must retain their original roster and assignment. Once the interruptions clear, both original armies defend Lageta. On a live replay, track the original army IDs through the contacts and look for any `Muster release` for another siege.
- **Viable relief before understrength fallback (`4a5fbf9b`):** both armies take the nearer Georgios camp while neither siege can be covered. A new, weaker Lageta camp becomes viable only with both armies. Block one route and they stay; restore it and reload, and both redirect intact. Once one army provides adequate local cover, only the spare transfers. A separate case chooses the earlier combined arrival over the earlier solo arrival. In campaign, `Relief route match` distinguishes `basis=adequate-response` from `basis=nearest-understrength`; neither implies victory.
- **Stable relief assignments (`f329d9d2`):** one army can cover Georgios and the other is understrength for its own objective. Small or alternating ETA leads must not swap their orders. The same clear advantage must last at least a planning interval before a speed-only swap; real loss of sufficient strength can move the armies immediately. A speed gain must not cost an adequately covered siege. In a live replay, compare `Relief ETA review` with the actual `Relief departure` lines.
- **Closing relief roster (`42c2579d`):** model Lageta holding and Saratis failing the intact-dispatch check for Jalmarys by blocking his direct route. Do not break up his army for a still-understrength muster. The same army enters idle grace, retains it across reload, and leaves free lords available to muster separately. A replacement that makes relief viable still works. If a closing formation cannot be released, `Relief release deferred` identifies the blocked parties and why, including a native battle or other control state.
- **Defense-only and army travel limit (`70259254`):** the 197-hour Lavenia route is ineligible. An offensive march loaded into a home siege is recalled and released if no relief job is reachable. An affordable, timely paid muster redirects intact. Existing assault protection is unchanged. While two home sieges overlap, ending only one does not reopen offense. A loaded over-limit relief march redirects intact or disperses without ending the objective. The route-limit and recall decisions appear as `Relief travel limit` and `home-siege-defense-only`.
- **East/west route choice:** either formation can cover the home castle alone, but only west has a route to the allied town. The production allocator sends east home and west to the ally. When the home camp requires both, both receive the native defend order on the same tick, the ally remains explicitly unassigned, and the first and combined readiness quotes differ.
- **Disruption and regrouping:** from two genuine production siege assignments, divert one and leave the other camp alone, or divert both. When neither formation can reach the defense directly, break up the necessary armies and gather a replacement via real modeled routes from their unchanged positions. The paid escort remains traveling rather than present until physical arrival, and the reserve debit does not replay after reload.
- **Native locks and independent parties:** leave an army in an active native assault even if its strength is needed; release it to defense only after the lock clears. Leaving one camp does not remove the independent besieger holding it. An invalid allied job with a battle-held traveler remains closing and protected while another army is assigned home; reload preserves both records independently.
- **Registry audit:** read relief and siege maps together, including closing work and pending escort cleanup, and check formation, objective, native order, leader, attached and traveling roles. A deliberately inconsistent fixture roster is detected even when native `party.Army` alone appears unique.
- **Reproduced defect:** an independent party inside both camps' response radii could be counted at home via its old defend order and at the allied camp via its current native engagement. The new regression failed before the fix: the eligible home army stayed idle. The fix credits the current battle or engagement first; ordinary selection and priorities are unchanged.
- **One-shot observer:** passing split, joint and diversion examples go through production planning. Negative fixtures reject different kingdoms, nonoverlapping orders, and offense-only activity. Reload of an old observer state cannot duplicate the popup or replay production orders.
- **Observer regression after the live run:** the three-army understrength transfer reproduced the missing verdict before the observer fix. Its two near-misses (fresh armies with no previous lower commitment, and previously ordered armies with no remaining lower route) stay inconclusive. No planner changes were needed.

For inspection, pair `Relief response selected` (first/combined ETA and comparison) with `Coordination commitment` (actual claimed objectives and party roles), `Relief review` (including `committed-higher-priority`), and native order, camp, release or withdrawal lines. `Coordination commitment` is emitted on changed assignments, not on each review. The live observer's `coordination proof` records the choice and observation times, the contemporaneous orders, and audited party identities.
